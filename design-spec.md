Design Spec
1. Mood
Vibrant, premium, welcoming, warm, modern.

2. Audience feel
"I can easily plan a fun, worry-free, and delicious night out with my favorite people."

3. Colors

Primary (Buttons/Highlights): Kyoto Vermilion Red (#E13E35) — punchy, appetizing, and culturally on-point.

Background: Rice Paper (#FDFBF7) — warm and premium; avoids the stark corporate look.

Accent (Text & Footer): Charcoal (#2C2C2C) — softer and more elegant than pure black.

4. Typography feel
Bold, rounded headings for a friendly vibe, paired with highly legible, clean text for the menu ingredients (maximizing convenience).

5. Layout direction
Airy and spacious. The Home page should lead with a large, warm photo. The Menu page should prioritize big, easy-to-tap filter buttons at the top so users on phones don't have to squint.

6. Component style
Friendly but refined. Buttons and menu cards should have gently rounded corners (e.g., border-radius: 8px;) and soft, subtle shadows so they look like they are floating slightly off the background. Absolutely no harsh, sharp, thick borders.

7. Images or icons
Use warm-toned, high-quality photos showing the experience (sizzling grills, warm lighting, ambient restaurant shots). Avoid sterile, bright-white stock photos of raw ingredients, and avoid anything that looks dark, shadowy, or gloomy. Use simple, minimalist icons for the "Vegan" or "Halal" tags if you decide to add them later.
